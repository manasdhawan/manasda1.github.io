Mortgage Rate Time Series Analysis

I analyzed the behavior of U.S. 30-year fixed mortgage rates using a range of time-series methods, including ARIMA, exponential smoothing, ARDL, GARCH models, and stochastic differential equations. I also investigated the relationship between mortgage rates and macroeconomic variables and produced long-term forecasts under several modeling assumptions.
