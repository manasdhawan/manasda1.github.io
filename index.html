Projects

Here are some of the projects I have worked on:

Mortgage Rate Time Series Analysis

I analyzed the behavior of U.S. mortgage rates using a range of time-series methods, including ARIMA, exponential smoothing, ARDL, and GARCH models. I also investigated the relationship between mortgage rates and macroeconomic variables and produced long-term forecasts under several modeling assumptions. View the project on GitHub.

Mortgage-Backed Securities Pricing

I am developing a framework for valuing mortgage-backed securities using loan-level cash-flow modeling, borrower prepayment behavior, default assumptions, interest-rate scenarios, and risk-adjusted performance measures. The project explores how mortgage pools can be compared across different economic and behavioral assumptions. View the project on GitHub.
